Why This Matters Before You List

The Cost of Mispricing. Pricing too high is the most common mistake sellers make — and the market penalizes it fast. Showings drop off after the first two weeks. Days on market accumulate. Buyers start wondering what's wrong with the property.

Price reductions signal desperation. A home that sat for 60 days and dropped $25,000 will close for less than one that was priced right and sold in two weeks — even if both end up at the same number on paper.

Pricing Too Low
Leaves real money on the table, often permanently.

Square Footage & FHA Compliance. The number on your tax record or Zillow listing may not reflect what a certified appraiser measures. Under ANSI Z765 — the national standard — only above-grade finished space counts as gross living area. Below-grade space, finished basements, and converted areas are classified and valued separately.

If you've added a room, finished a bonus space, or converted a garage since you bought the property, that square footage needs to be verified before you list. A discrepancy found mid-transaction can delay or kill a deal.

FHA financing adds another layer. Certain property conditions — deferred maintenance, missing components, safety issues — must be flagged in FHA appraisals. If your buyer is using FHA financing and the property doesn't meet guidelines, the deal stalls. Vaughn can identify those risks before they become surprises.

Ready to get started? Just have the address ready. If there's anything unusual about the property — additions, conversions, tenant occupancy, access issues — mention it when you call. Everything else gets handled in the conversation.
