The Full Picture
of This Industry

Most appraisers know one side of this business. Vaughn knows all of them. Before earning his certification, Vaughn worked inside an established appraisal firm in multiple roles — managing the appraiser panel, working the appraisal desk assigning orders, and bringing sales experience to client relationships. That breadth of exposure is rare, and it shapes how Vaughn Valuation operates every day.

Working on the desk means Vaughn has personally felt the downstream impact of a missed turn time or a slow revision response. He knows what it costs a lender when an order stalls, and what it costs an AMC when a report comes back needing work. That institutional perspective isn't something you get from appraisal school — it comes from doing the job on both sides of the transaction.

After moving into the field as a trainee, Vaughn built a reputation for taking on complex, luxury, and waterfront assignments that others passed on — and for responding to revision requests quickly and without friction. Last year, Vaughn led his firm in production, a result of consistent quality, reliable turn times, and a client-first approach to every engagement.

Now with Vaughn Valuation, that same standard applies independently — with the added flexibility to serve lenders, AMCs, and private clients across North Texas and the Texoma region with the attention and accountability each engagement deserves.

Outside of work, Vaughn is a former Division II collegiate swimmer — a background that instilled the discipline and consistency that define how he approaches his work. He and his wife are based in the Texoma area.
